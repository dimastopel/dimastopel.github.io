An experiment with something funny this time. We have a reality now that everything is available via subscription. You can’t own anything but you can lease services. Imagine that the next thing that is going to be available via subscription is a toilet. You will have regular, pro, and max subscriptions. With regular you will be limited to a single number two session per day. Once the limit is reached the toilet plays a sad sound and locks the seat. Had Indian food? You can purchase an emergency day pass for $4.99 or go to neighbors. With the pro subscription, you can take as much shit as you want but you share the same infrastructure with other pro subscribers in your area. So mornings will be challenging. With the max subscription you get your own sewer that is installed at your house that connects you to the main sewerage and you can now stop worrying about that double-wrapped burrito from yesterday. Have another one. You also get your own account manager available to you 24/7. Use the toilet between 2 and 5 AM and save 15%. Towards Christmas you get a year-end summary email showing your stats and peak usage times.

I decided to try microblogging to make my mind more flexible in this regard. For some reason writing always attracted me but I never persevered with it. I do love reading and I still feel that no other media succeeded in creating such a rich world in your mind while consuming it. I think this is the reason why it is so hard to create a movie based on a true bestseller. Hopefully, with time, I’ll be able to write longer and more interesting content more easily.  

I do not plan to use LLM for these posts at all as the whole purpose of this is to make my mind better at this. Anyway, I will try to invest half an hour every day to try and write something. We will see how it goes.
